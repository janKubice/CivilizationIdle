## 3) Full description (paste into the page body)

**Civdle** is a cozy-but-deep idle city-builder that runs in your
browser. Start by clicking trees and rocks for your first resources, raise a few
huts, and put your people to work. From there your settlement grows on its own —
into a village, a town, a metropolis, and finally a laser-age **Ecumenopolis** that
sprawls across an infinite, procedurally generated world.

It's one self-contained HTML file. **No install, no accounts, works offline, and
autosaves** as you play.

### 🌍 A living, endless world
- Infinite procedurally generated map — forests, hills, deserts, lakes, mountains.
- A city that feels alive: people walk to work and back, carts, boats, trains,
  cars and trucks on asphalt roads, birds, day/night, and four seasons that change
  the land and the economy.
- **Terraform** water, mountains and desert into buildable land — expand forever.

### ⚙️ Deep, satisfying systems
- A **tech tree of 40+ technologies** from stone tools to fusion, robotics and
  orbital lasers.
- **Building levels, 4-in-1 merging into grand buildings, and districts** that
  reward clustering.
- **World Wonders** (the Great Pyramid, Steel Tower, Space Elevator, Great Wall)
  that take time to build and grant civilization-wide powers.
- **Army & raids**: defend your city with walls, barracks, watchtowers and missile
  bases — a warning banner tells you if you'll hold.
- **Automation** when you want it: a Governor that builds by itself, auto-merge,
  auto-upgrade and a foreman — set it up and watch the city run.
- **Prestige / Ascension** with a deep, repeatable perk tree that keeps unlocking.

### 🏙️ Feel the power (megalomania included)
- **City ranks** from Hamlet to Ecumenopolis, each with a permanent production bonus.
- A **Space Program** with rockets launching to the stars, a **nuclear plant**, and
  **orbital lasers** that strike from the sky for huge windfalls.

### 🎯 Easy to start, hard to put down
- **Goals** guide you from your very first minute, with rewards.
- **Click combos**, golden citizens, festivals, meteors, a travelling circus, and
  choice events keep things lively.
- **Live stats & graphs** and generative music that evolves through the ages.

### 🕹️ Play your way
- Mouse, touch and keyboard. Works great on desktop and mobile browsers.
- Available in **English, Czech, German and French**.
- Export/import your save as a code — take your civilization anywhere.

> Built as a single, dependency-free HTML file: every sprite is drawn in code and
> every sound is synthesized — nothing is downloaded, so it just works, even offline.

**How to play:** Click resources to gather them → build **[B]** → assign jobs **[P]**
→ meet your people's needs → new citizens arrive → research **[T]** and buy upgrades
**[U]**. Drag or WASD to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, click any building for details.
**Esc** closes menus. The 🔭 button zooms all the way out to admire your empire.

## 4) Controls (short block for the page)

- **Click** a tree / rock / bush / ore — gather resources (chain clicks for a combo!)
- **B** — Build · **P** — Jobs · **T** — Science · **U** — Upgrades · **Esc** — close
- **Drag** or **WASD / arrows** — pan · **Mouse wheel / pinch** — zoom · **🔭** — overview
- **Click a building** — details, upgrade, merge, demolish
- Catch the **🌟 golden citizen** when it appears!

---

## 5) Uploading the HTML build to itch.io

1. Upload `civdle.html` (rename to `index.html` first, or zip it with
   that name — itch runs `index.html` automatically).
2. Tick **"This file will be played in the browser"**.
3. Set the viewport to about **1280 × 800**, and enable **"Fullscreen button"** and
   **"Mobile friendly"** (the game is responsive and touch-ready).
4. **SharedArrayBuffer is NOT required** — leave that option off.
5. Save & view page. That's it — the whole game is in that one file.

# 🇨🇿 Česká verze

**Krátký popis (tagline):**
> Vybuduj civilizaci od jednoho ohniště po laserové megaměsto. Klikej, stav,
> zkoumej — a nech své lidičky makat. Na nekonečné procedurální mapě.

**Plný popis:**

**Civdle** je útulné, ale hluboké idle budování města přímo v prohlížeči.
Začneš klikáním na stromy a kameny pro první suroviny, postavíš pár chatrčí a pošleš
lidi do práce. Odtud město roste samo — ve vesnici, město, metropoli a nakonec
laserovou **Ekumenopoli** rozlévající se přes nekonečný, procedurálně generovaný svět.

Je to jeden samostatný HTML soubor. **Bez instalace, bez účtů, funguje offline a
ukládá se sám.**

**Živý nekonečný svět** — nekonečná mapa (lesy, kopce, poušť, jezera, hory); lidé
chodí do práce, vozíky, loďky, vlaky, auta a náklaďáky na asfaltkách, ptáci, den/noc
a čtyři roční období. **Terraformuj** vodu, hory i poušť v úrodnou zem a rozpínej se
donekonečna.

**Hluboké systémy** — tech strom 40+ technologií od kamene po fúzi, robotiku a
orbitální lasery; úrovně budov, slučování 4-v-1 do velkých budov, čtvrti, **Divy
světa**; **armáda a nájezdy** (hradby, kasárna, věže, raketové základny s varováním,
zda se ubráníš); **automatizace** (Guvernér staví sám, auto-slučování, auto-upgrade,
Předák); **Vzestup** s hlubokým stromem perků.

**Cítit sílu** — hodnosti města od Osady po Ekumenopoli, každá s trvalým bonusem;
**vesmírný program** s raketami, **jaderná elektrárna** a **lasery z nebe**.

**Snadný start** — cíle tě vedou od první minuty, klik comba, zlatí občané, festivaly,
meteory, cirkus a eventy s volbou; **živé grafy** a **generativní hudba** měnící se
érou. Ovládání myší, dotykem i klávesnicí. **Čeština, angličtina, němčina, francouzština.**

**Jak hrát:** klikej na suroviny → stav **[B]** → přiřaď práci **[P]** → plň potřeby →
přicházejí noví lidé → zkoumej **[T]** a kupuj vylepšení **[U]**. Tažení / WASD = posun,
kolečko = zoom, 🔭 = přehled celého impéria, klik na budovu = detail.
